VFD-RT00-03 ASSEMBLY GUIDE, V3.0
Westby Creamery, 10006 Intake Receiving VFD. From "10006 VFD-RT00-03.pdf" (Sharpe Engineering, initial release 06/24/26).

V3.0 (Bob's review of V2): the incoming ground now lands on the ground lug GNDB0101, with a bond from the lug to
the ground bar (item 10). The drives' control terminal block is rebuilt: the upper row stands forward and its wires
come down in front of the lower row, not through it.
V2.0: makers' own 3D models for the drives, disconnect, fuse block, motor breakers, 24 V supply, 1489 breakers
and duct. The fuse block is now where Rockwell's instruction puts it (item 13). B01421 moved to terminal 4.
It has not been checked against a built panel, and it rests on the readings listed below.
Read "Issues" in the guide before you build from it.

HOW TO OPEN IT
  Double-click "VFD-RT00-03 Assembly Guide.html". It opens in Edge or Chrome. It needs an internet connection.

WHAT THE SCREEN DOES
  Top:     Build (step by step), Wire (one wire at a time), Parts, Wire list, Issues. Find box: type B01081, TB1, CBB0108.
  Bottom:  Back / Next step. In Wire: Done, next wire.
  Left:    Finished (the completed panel), Doors off, Reset, Closer / Further, Colours, Wide view.
  Click any part or wire to see what it is, where it lands and how long to cut it. It reopens at the step you left.

WHERE THE GUIDE IS NOT AS THE DRAWINGS SHOW IT (each is also on the wire or part, and under Issues)
  1. Tags. The 24 V supply and its fuse holder carry the layout tags PWSB0125 and FUB0125 (the schematic says
     PWSB0132 and FUB0132). The three Ethernet jacks have no tag on the drawings: JKN0102, JKN0104, JKN0106 here.
     The fuse block under the disconnect is DISCB0101-F here.
  2. Terminal numbers. The drawings do not number the terminals. This guide numbers each strip from the left:
       TB120-1: 1-4 B01421, 5-7 C01021 (jumpered), 8 C01022, 9 C01051, 10 C01081, 11-13 B01411 (jumpered), 14-17 ground
       TB1:     1-3 B02061, 4 B02101, 5-7 B03061, 8 B03101, 9-11 B04061, 12 B04101, 13-19 B01341, 20-23 ground
                (each group of three and the seven B01341 blocks are jumpered)
       TB24-1:  1-2 B01331 (jumpered), 3 B01341
     Panel wiring is on the upper screw, field wiring on the lower. Where a block takes two panel wires the second is
     on the lower screw. Which block and which side each wire uses is this guide's choice.
     B01421 uses terminal 4: from the transformer fuse on the lower screw, to CBC0102 on the upper.
  3. "1AWG". Ten branch conductors printed 1AWG on sheet B01 are shown as 14 AWG (the next phase, and the same wires
     on the drive sheets, are printed 14AWG). B01021, B01022, B01023 (fuse block to TBB0106) are left at 1 AWG as
     printed. CONFIRM ALL SIZES BEFORE CUTTING.
  4. B01421 and B01411 are printed 8 AWG into TB120-1. A 1492-J4 block takes 22 to 10 AWG. Shown as printed. CONFIRM.
  5. Transformer fuses. FUB0137 and FUB0142 are the transformer's own fuse blocks. Three conductors the schematic
     draws (fuse to H1, fuse to H4, X1 to fuse) are listed "not a wire to make". The H2-H3 link is the flat link.
  6. Pump feedback returns. Sheet C01's terminal labels do not agree with sheet Z04. The guide follows Z04:
     CRAUX1 returns on terminal 8 (C01022), CRAUX2 on 9 (C01051), CRAUX3 on 10 (C01081).
  7. Centre jumpers, not wire jumpers, on the B0x061 groups (sheet Z05 draws the jumper; B02-B04 print a wire size).
  8. Relay terminals. Only sheet B04 prints 11 and 14 on the contact; all three relays are wired that way.
     WHICH SCREW ON THE RELAY IS WHICH IS ASSUMED: check the marking on the relay.
  9. Fuse holders and 1489 breakers: line at the top is this guide's choice.
 10. GROUND. The drawing takes the incoming ground to a junction with no terminal. The guide makes that junction on
     the ground lug GNDB0101: incoming ground in its middle opening, a bond from its left opening to tap 1 of the
     ground bar GNDB0100. Neither has a size on the drawings (the bond is shown as 8 AWG). CONFIRM.
 11. SAFE-OFF. The drive sheets say "Remove safe-off jumper" but connect nothing to S1 and S2. The guide lands
     nothing there. LEAVE THE SAFE-OFF JUMPER IN until engineering says what S1/S2 connect to.
 12. Field wiring (feeder, motor leads, pump contacts, Ethernet to the switch) has no drawn entry. It is shown
     leaving through the floor. Motor wiring beyond the auxiliary disconnects is not shown.

 13. FUSE BLOCK HEIGHT. Sheet Z02 draws the fuse block about 70 mm lower than a 60 A class J fuse reaches (it draws
     the 100 A spacing). The guide puts it by Rockwell's instruction: block mounting holes 1.976 in (50.2 mm) below
     the switch's lower mounting holes. Set it with an LPJ-60SP fuse in hand.

WHAT IS STILL A STAND-IN OR A SIMPLE SHAPE
  The enclosure (an 18 in deep model pressed to 12 in) and the air conditioner are stand-ins. Fuse holders, relays
  and Ethernet jacks are simple shapes. The drive's power terminal block is built to the larger frame's pattern.
  Use the terminal NAMES on the wire card to find a terminal.

AUDIT: no wire through a solid, across a device or sharing a clamp; every wire end lands on a device that exists.
No tight bends.

VFD 200 ASSEMBLY GUIDE - START HERE
===================================

OPEN IT
  1. Download the "Claude Digital Twin" folder from Google Drive, or just
     the file "VFD200 Assembly Guide.html".
  2. Double-click "VFD200 Assembly Guide.html". It opens in your browser
     (Chrome or Edge). It needs internet access for the 3D library.

USE IT
  - Work through the steps with "Next step" (or the arrow keys).
    Tick each task as you finish it; the ticks are remembered on this
    computer.
  - Drag to turn the panel, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
  - Click a part to see its part number, install notes and links.
  - Click a wire to see its number, size, both ends and the drawing note.
  - "Wires" tab: the full wire list. "Download marker list (CSV)" gives a
    file for the label printer.
  - "Issues" tab: drawing and BOM questions to raise with engineering.
  - The PartsLib links open the manual at the right page. Sign in with
    your cpme-usa.com Google account.

WHAT'S IN THE FOLDER
  VFD200 Assembly Guide.html   The guide. This is the only file a
                               builder needs.
  README.md                    What the guide contains, what is assumed,
                               and how to rebuild it.
  COMPLETION.md                One-page summary of this pass.
  src\, data\, assets\,        Source for the guide (developers only).
  vendor\, tools\, build.mjs

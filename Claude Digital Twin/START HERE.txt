VFD 200 SIMULATOR - START HERE
==============================

ONCE ONLY
  Install Node.js from https://nodejs.org (the LTS version).

EACH TIME
  1. Download the "Claude Digital Twin" folder from Google Drive.
  2. Right-click the downloaded zip and choose "Extract All".
  3. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Start SCADA Sim.cmd".
     If Windows warns you, click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  4. Two browser tabs open:
       - SCADA screen: run the pumps, open valves, trigger faults.
       - 3D twin: the panel, live. Drag to rotate; use the buttons along
         the bottom to open the doors, cut it away or explode it.
  5. To stop it, close the black command window.

NOTES
  - The 3D twin needs internet access (it loads its 3D library online).
  - Don't move files out of the folder; they need each other.
  - The .cmd won't run from the Drive web preview, so extract the folder first.


WHAT'S IN THE PACKAGE
=====================

  Start SCADA Sim.cmd   The launcher. Double-click to run everything.

  scada.html            The SCADA screen: CIP process with clickable valves,
                        power one-line, 24 V breaker channels, drive
                        faceplates, panel heat, trends, alarms and
                        instructor faults.

  panel-twin.html       The 3D twin of the panel, laid out from the drawings.
                        It also opens on its own (double-click) without the
                        simulator.

  scada-sim.mjs         The simulator server. It serves both pages and runs
                        Modbus TCP on port 5021 for testing real SCADA or HMI
                        software against it.

  panel-model.mjs       The engineering model behind both pages: drives,
                        pumps, breakers, E-stop, 24 V channels and panel heat.
                        Device powers, losses and pump curves are listed at
                        the top for swapping in datasheet values.

  README.md             Full documentation: the design review findings, what
                        is assumed, and the complete Modbus register map.

  COMPLETION.md         A one-page summary of what was built and the top five
                        findings.

  out\panel-vfd200.mp4  A 28-second video of the twin.

  build.mjs             Developer tool to rebuild the twin after editing the
                        model. It needs a toolkit that is only on Bob's PC, so
                        others can ignore it.

VFD 200 ASSEMBLY GUIDE V12.0 - START HERE
=========================================

OPEN IT
  Double-click "VFD200 Assembly Guide.html" (Chrome or Edge). It needs
  internet access for the 3D library. The file is about 14 MB: give it a
  few seconds to load. It opens at the step you left it at.

NEW IN V12
  E-STOP safety wiring: W24 is a jumper on TB5 from terminal 1 to 3, and
  W25 from terminal 2 to 4, on the door side of the strip. One wire goes
  from TB5-3 to E-STOP 12 and one from TB5-4 to E-STOP 21. (V11 ran all
  four to the E-STOP.) Sheet 5 does not say where the junctions are made;
  this is the guide's choice and is listed for engineering to confirm.

THE SCREEN
  Along the top   Build, Wire, Parts, Wire list, Issues; how many tasks and
                  wires are done; Find (type W29, CB4, TB4, FU1-L1); AA for
                  larger text; ? for help.
  On the 3D view  Finished (the completed panel), Doors off, Reset, Closer,
                  Further, Colours (what each wire colour is; pick one to
                  show only those), Wide view (hides the instructions).
                  Under the view: Look from front, left, right, above, below.
  At the bottom   One row of big buttons that never moves: Back / Next step
                  in Build; Back / Done, next wire / Skip in Wire.

MOVE AROUND
  Drag to turn the panel, all the way round and underneath. Scroll or pinch
  to zoom right in to a terminal. Right-drag to slide the view.
  Keys: arrows = step or wire, C = Finished, F = Doors off, R = Reset,
  T = fly along the selected wire, K = Colours, P = Wide view, H = help,
  + and - = zoom, Esc = clear the selection.

BUILD
  Each step shows where you are in the job, what to do as tick rows, what to
  check with engineering first, and the parts fitted in that step. Parts and
  documents are folded away until you open them. Next step turns green when
  every task in the step is ticked. Ticks are remembered on this computer.

WIRE
  Press "Start wiring" on a wiring step, or Wire at the top. Each card shows
  the marker, size, colour and cut length, both ends (device, terminal, where
  it is on the device, end type, torque and strip length) and the duct route.
  Press "Done, next wire" (or Enter) after each one.
  Wire list: marker list (CSV) for the label printer, a printable run sheet
  with tick boxes, and a cut list with metres per wire size.

SELECT AND TRACE
  Click a wire: everything else turns to glass and both ends are ringed and
  named. Click a part: its wires stay solid and every terminal is tagged.
  The buttons on the selection are Wire it, Wires, Parts, Step, Trace route.

THINGS THAT ARE NOT AS THE DRAWINGS SHOW THEM
  FU1         A trailer fuse block, 113 mm under Q1. The three LPJ-70SP
              fuses are the link from Q1 to FU1: there are no W1-W3 wires.
  T1          H3 to H2 is the flat metal link that comes with the
              transformer, not a wire.
  RCP1        One terminal block (L, N, G) at the top feeds both outlets.
              The two jumpers on sheet 6 are not fitted. Check the L/N/G
              marking on the device.
  TB5         W24 and W25 are jumpers on the strip (above). W24, W25 and
              W102 are marked on the door side of TB5 only.
  Rail 3      PLC1, PWS2, IO1 from the left, as built (sheet 3 draws PWS2
              first). IO1 is 52.5 mm wide.
  CBL-IOL-09  Lands on CPL2 X01-1, X01-3, X01-4 (sheet 11 misprints two).
  Feeder      The incoming feeder (Q1 1L1, 3L2, 5L3) and ground (GB1
              terminal 1) are by the installer; size and entry point are
              not on the drawings.
  Fuses       T1's three fuses are not in the BOM; the guide shows a best
              approximation (FNQ-R-3-1/2 x 2, FNM-10 x 1). Confirm before
              ordering. Three LPJ-70SP are needed; the BOM lists one.
  Every one of these is under Issues, for the engineer who drew the panel.

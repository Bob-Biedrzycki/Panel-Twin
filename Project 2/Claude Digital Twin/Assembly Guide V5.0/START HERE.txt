VFD 200 ASSEMBLY GUIDE V5.0 - START HERE
========================================

OPEN IT
  Double-click "VFD200 Assembly Guide.html" (Chrome or Edge). It needs
  internet access for the 3D library. The file is about 14 MB: give it a
  few seconds to load the makers' 3D models.

SEE THE FINISHED PANEL
  - "Completed panel" (or C) shows the panel as it will look when it is
    done: every part and wire in place, doors and centre post off, duct
    covers off, drive covers and 1769 doors open so every landing shows.
    Press C again, or any step, to go back.

BUILD
  - Go through the steps with "Next step" or the arrow keys. Tick each task
    as you finish it; ticks are remembered on this computer.
  - Drag to turn the panel (all the way round and underneath), scroll to
    zoom in close to a terminal, right-drag to pan.
  - "Remove front" (or F) takes the doors and the centre post off so you
    can see inside. "Reset view" (or R) goes back to the step's view.
  - Click any part for its part number, install notes, PartsLib links, where
    its 3D shape comes from, and a table of every terminal and its wire.

SELECT AND TRACE
  - Click a wire: everything else turns to glass, both terminals are ringed
    and labelled. Press T (or Trace route) to fly along it.
  - Click a part: its wires stay solid and every terminal gets a tag.
  - The bar at the top of the panel jumps to the same wire or part in Build
    steps, Wire it, Parts or Wires. Esc clears. The search box finds W29,
    CB4, TB4...

WIRE
  - On a wiring step, press "Wire them one by one" (or open "Wire it").
    Each card gives the marker, size, colour and cut length, both ends
    (device, terminal, where it is on the device, end type, torque and
    strip length) and the duct route.
  - Press "Done, next" (or Enter) after each wire. Progress is saved.
  - Wires tab: marker list (CSV) for the label printer, a printable run
    sheet with tick boxes, and a cut list with metres per wire size.

RCP1 (receptacle)
  - The 1492-REC15 has ONE terminal block (L, N, G) at the top that feeds
    both outlets. Land hot on L, neutral on N, ground on G. The two jumpers
    drawn on sheet 6 are not fitted. Check the L/N/G marking on the device.

ISSUES
  - Design issues: questions for the engineer who drew the panel.
  - Modeling issues: what this guide assumes, and what it has resolved.

VFD 200 ASSEMBLY GUIDE V11.0 - START HERE
=========================================

NEW IN V11
  The wiring diagram (sheets 4-15) was checked against the guide wire by
  wire. Added: the incoming feeder to Q1 (1L1, 3L2, 5L3) and the incoming
  ground to GB1 terminal 1, both by the installer on site.
  T1: H3 to H2 is the flat metal link that comes with the transformer,
  not a wire.
  W24, W25 and W102 are marked on the door side of TB5 only.
  When something is selected the buttons read: Wire it, Wires, Parts,
  Step, Trace route.

NEW IN V10
  PWS1 (WAGO Pro 2): the 24 V output wires go down into the blue plug on
  top of the supply (X2: 1-3 minus, 4-5 plus). The 480 V input and its
  ground go up into the black plug under the bottom (X1: L1 L2 L3 PE).
  Nothing lands on the front.
  Every fuse is a part you can click or find: FU1-L1, FU1-L2, FU1-L3,
  FU2-F, T1-F1, T1-F2, T1-F3. T1's fuses are not in the BOM: the guide
  shows a best approximation.

NEW IN V9
  The screen is rebuilt for the person at the bench. Along the top: Build,
  Wire, Parts, Wire list, Issues, how much is done, and Find. At the bottom
  of the instructions: one row of big buttons (Back / Next step, or Back /
  Done, next wire / Skip). On the 3D view: Finished, Doors off, Reset,
  Closer, Further, Colours, Wide view. AA makes the text larger. The guide
  opens at the step you left it at.
  FU1 is a trailer fuse block: it hangs 113 mm under Q1 and the three
  LPJ-70SP fuses are the link. There are no W1-W3 wires to make.
  (The button and tab names further down this file are V8's.)

NEW IN V8
  Q1, FU1 and PDB1 are wired straight lug to lug. Wires that share a clamp
  lie side by side. IO1's wires leave through its wire channel and PWS2's
  come from below. CBL-IOL-09 lands on one port (X01-1, -3, -4).
  "Completed panel" now fits the whole plate in the window. New buttons:
  Look from (front, left, right, above, below), Wire colours (K), Help (H).
  P hides the side panel.

NEW IN V7
  IO1 wires come in from the bottom. FU1 load wires go into their lugs. The
  isolator plugs are stepped, so the 9-12 wires clear the 13-16 plug. The
  drive covers stay on as see-through glass in the wiring steps and the
  Completed panel.

NEW IN V6
  Checked against the shop photos of the build (2026-11-05). The CompactLogix
  goes on as PLC1, PWS2, IO1 from the left (sheet 3 draws PWS2 first). The
  drive, PB2, OW16, NS1, isolator, T1 and PDB1 terminals are where they are on
  the fitted parts. See REVISIONS.md for the full list.

OPEN IT
  Double-click "VFD200 Assembly Guide.html" (Chrome or Edge). It needs
  internet access for the 3D library. The file is about 14 MB: give it a
  few seconds to load the makers' 3D models.

SEE THE FINISHED PANEL
  - "Completed panel" (or C) shows the panel as it will look when it is
    done: every part and wire in place, doors and centre post off, duct
    covers off, drive covers on as see-through glass and 1769 doors open,
    so every landing shows.
    Press C again, or any step, to go back.

BUILD
  - Go through the steps with "Next step" or the arrow keys. Tick each task
    as you finish it; ticks are remembered on this computer.
  - Drag to turn the panel (all the way round and underneath), scroll to
    zoom in close to a terminal, right-drag to pan.
  - "Remove front" (or F) takes the doors and the centre post off so you
    can see inside. "Reset view" (or R) goes back to the step's view.
  - Click any part for its part number, install notes, PartsLib links, where
    its 3D shape comes from, and a table of every terminal and its wire.

SELECT AND TRACE
  - Click a wire: everything else turns to glass, both terminals are ringed
    and labelled. Press T (or Trace route) to fly along it.
  - Click a part: its wires stay solid and every terminal gets a tag.
  - The bar at the top of the panel jumps to the same wire or part in Build
    steps, Wire it, Parts or Wires. Esc clears. The search box finds W29,
    CB4, TB4...

WIRE
  - On a wiring step, press "Wire them one by one" (or open "Wire it").
    Each card gives the marker, size, colour and cut length, both ends
    (device, terminal, where it is on the device, end type, torque and
    strip length) and the duct route.
  - Press "Done, next" (or Enter) after each wire. Progress is saved.
  - Wires tab: marker list (CSV) for the label printer, a printable run
    sheet with tick boxes, and a cut list with metres per wire size.

RCP1 (receptacle)
  - The 1492-REC15 has ONE terminal block (L, N, G) at the top that feeds
    both outlets. Land hot on L, neutral on N, ground on G. The two jumpers
    drawn on sheet 6 are not fitted. Check the L/N/G marking on the device.
